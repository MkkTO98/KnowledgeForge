Campaigns 1-6 validate deterministic WDI demographic production across evidence-quality, completeness, freshness, inventory, territorial, and temporal coverage scopes without architecture change.

## Does Campaign 6 add new capability?

Campaign 6 adds genuinely new production capability: temporal applicability and historical period coverage matrices were not previously produced. It also increases confidence in the existing deterministic production pipeline.

## Forward-looking maturity assessment

Completion of Campaign 7 is likely to satisfy the provenance-lineage portion of Mature status for this family if it demonstrates complete lineage/fingerprint handling, stable rejected-candidate preservation for malformed provenance, and no architecture change.

Remaining assumptions before broadening into another evidence family:

- accepted objects still use one evidence reference each;
- later-stage production rejection remains weakly exercised;
- partial provenance disagreement remains untested;
- non-demographic WDI evidence-family transfer remains untested and should be Campaign 8's role.

## Roadmap implication

Campaign 7 should proceed unchanged. Mature status should be assessed after Campaign 7, not asserted now.
