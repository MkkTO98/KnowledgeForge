# Long-Term Production Roadmap — Phase 2

Date: 2026-07-09
Status: completed planning artifact

## Roadmap posture

This roadmap is family-oriented, not campaign-oriented. It defines the Phase 2 production expansion sequence without designing individual campaigns.

Every family should use the Production Methodology Closeout criteria unchanged:

1. evidence-quality transfer;
2. inventory/classification where applicable;
3. territorial and temporal coverage where applicable;
4. rejected-candidate preservation;
5. deterministic replay and stable fingerprints;
6. dedicated provenance-lineage completeness;
7. family closeout report before Mature status.

## Phase 2 sequence

### Stage 1 — Controlled third-family broadening

Target family: WDI Infrastructure annual-scalar evidence.

Purpose: prove that the methodology remains stable for a third distinct WDI family and begin Phase 2 with the lowest-risk high-value expansion.

Expected learning: third-family transfer, structural knowledge, coverage/provenance repeatability, and continued no-architecture-change evidence.

### Stage 2 — Technical-domain heterogeneity

Target family: WDI Energy & Mining annual-scalar evidence.

Purpose: add a technical/non-demographic/non-environmental family that is still bounded enough for deterministic maturation.

Expected learning: technical-domain vocabulary handling, boundary scanning for causal/policy wording, and additional family closeout evidence.

### Stage 3 — Small-family contrast

Target family: WDI Agriculture & Rural Development annual-scalar evidence.

Purpose: test whether the methodology remains efficient and meaningful for narrower families rather than only broad families.

Expected learning: maturity criteria proportionality, negative knowledge patterns in smaller families, and closeout discipline.

### Stage 4 — Broader WDI cross-family comparison

Target: cross-family comparison across three or four WDI Mature/near-Mature families.

Purpose: increase multi-reference evidence, overlap handling, duplicate pressure, and provenance-difference evidence beyond the two-family Campaign 9 case.

Expected learning: whether multi-reference and cross-family comparison remain stable under larger family count.

### Stage 5 — Higher boundary-risk WDI families

Target families: WDI Trade and WDI Financial Sector.

Purpose: mature objective evidence-level knowledge in families whose domain vocabulary creates higher risk of economic, policy, investment, or recommendation interpretation.

Expected learning: validator/boundary robustness under higher-risk terminology while preserving non-interpretive constraints.

### Stage 6 — Scale-family stress

Target family: WDI Education or WDI Health.

Purpose: test practical scale, catalogue volume, object similarity, and summary/report maintainability after smaller/mid-sized families establish expected behaviour.

Expected learning: whether dozens of production families and high-indicator-count families create real production pressure.

### Stage 7 — Non-WDI multi-source disagreement workstream

Target: one deliberately selected non-WDI source family that can be compared against already matured WDI evidence without turning KnowledgeForge into an ingestion system.

Purpose: exercise the remaining major falsification gap: source disagreement, partial provenance disagreement, and external-source heterogeneity.

Expected learning: whether existing contracts and validators handle non-WDI disagreement without redesign.

## Stop/review gates

Run a planning review before leaving WDI annual-scalar evidence if any of the following occur:

- repeated validator false positives or false negatives;
- repeated package-contract friction;
- duplicate pressure that campaign-local checks cannot manage;
- provenance/fingerprint ambiguity not handled by existing package fields;
- sustained manual candidate-screening cost across multiple families;
- failures in final coherence or production-quality reporting that are not environment issues.

None of these conditions is currently met.

## Architectural continuity conclusion

This roadmap preserves the agreed architecture and validated methodology. It does not authorize architecture, taxonomy, validator, package, runtime, database, adapter, API, repository-coupling, local-model, or frontier-model changes.
