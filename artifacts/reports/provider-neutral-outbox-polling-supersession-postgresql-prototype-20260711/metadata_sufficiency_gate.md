# Metadata Sufficiency Gate

A. Known registered indicator and compatible retained semantics: automatic deterministic recomputation permitted.

B. Known indicator but changed short label/unit: semantic review required; quarantine for automatic processing.

C. New unseen indicator with short label only: candidate registry proposal allowed; automatic relationship generation prohibited; full provider definition/source metadata required before activation.

D. Full definition/source metadata available and validated: eligible for deterministic applicability screening.

Rules: do not infer full definitions from short labels; do not use local AI to fill missing provider semantics.
