# Pearson-Family Status Decision

Decision: Declare the Pearson correlation production family Stable, not Mature.

Justification:

- Repeated production evidence exists across Campaigns 36-39.
- Offline no-promote reproduction now covers all 7 historical Pearson packages through declarative specs.
- Semantic diversity improved: trade, demographic, health, infrastructure.
- Entity diversity covers DNK, SWE, and NOR.
- PostgreSQL generic retrieval is sufficient.
- Governance overhead is improving but still requires monitoring.
- Missingness/post-freeze rejection experience remains thin.
- Downstream usefulness has not been consumption-tested.
- Candidate selection still needs a registry-backed model before high-volume production.

Status: Stable means controlled production may continue after a separate authorization gate. Mature is deferred until a bounded family-maturation assessment validates candidate registry, rejection handling, governance ratio, downstream retrieval usefulness, and scalable no-promote reproduction.
