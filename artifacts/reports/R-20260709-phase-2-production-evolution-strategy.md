## Phase 2 PEL use

Continue the current PEL methodology unchanged.

For each new family, record only production evidence from outputs, validation reports, rejected catalogues, production-quality reports, retrospectives, architecture observations, and closeout reports.

Keep observations in `monitor` unless repeated production evidence justifies investigation. Move to `investigate` only for a bounded planning/proof task. Move to `implement` only after investigation plus later campaign evidence proves a minimal change preserves the architecture and reduces repeated production friction.

## Additional Phase 2 monitoring tags

- family-count scaling;
