## Campaign 4 value as falsification step

Campaign 4 should proceed because it directly targets several current weak spots without changing architecture:

- richer inventory classification;
- supported/unsupported dimensions;
- more similar objects;
- duplicate pressure;
- category assignment consistency;
- factual/classified coverage.

## Gaps not fully covered by Campaign 4

Campaign 4 will not fully test:

- multi-evidence-reference accepted objects;
- cross-family comparisons;
- production artifacts as evidence;
- local-model-assisted candidate drafts;
- later-stage validator rejection under model/draft pressure.

These gaps are already later-roadmap items or conditional future items. They do not require insertion before Campaign 4.

## Roadmap recommendation

Proceed directly to Campaign 4 unchanged.

Do not insert a stress campaign before Campaign 4. The roadmap already contains a coherent falsification sequence, and Campaign 4 itself is the correct next stressor.
