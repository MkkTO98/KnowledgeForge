# KnowledgeForge Foundation Era Timeline

Date: 2026-07-09
Status: canonical historical timeline for Phase 1 / Foundation Era

## Purpose

This timeline is the canonical concise historical record of KnowledgeForge's Foundation Era. It records the sequence of objectives, accomplishments, decisions, completion evidence, and milestones that led to Operational Expansion.

| Stage | Objective | Major accomplishments | Architectural decisions | Evidence required for completion | Resulting milestone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial conception | Define KnowledgeForge's independent EIP role. | Established KnowledgeForge as the reusable knowledge substrate answering "What is known?" | KnowledgeForge owns reusable knowledge, not observations, reasoning, forecasting, recommendations, reports, visualization, or presentation. | Constitution and foundational architecture accepted. | Independent constitutional boundary established. |
| Architectural consolidation | Convert early conceptual vocabulary into durable architecture. | Consolidated identity/content/applicability/evidence/evolution/governance components; first-class claims; representation neutrality. | Knowledge objects are governed reusable claims/facets, not graph/database/API-defined entities. | Accepted architecture and decisions preserved without runtime implementation. | Provisional specification freeze and coherent architecture baseline. |
| Package/provenance/fingerprint specification | Define reproducible knowledge packages. | Specified package kinds, evidence references, computation method, generated statements, provenance envelope, fingerprints, validation state, evolution metadata. | Packages are conceptual contracts, not database schemas. | Package/provenance/fingerprint docs established audit requirements. | Reproducible package contract baseline. |
| Validation framework creation | Prove deterministic contract enforcement. | Created deterministic validator taxonomy and Validation Framework v1. | Validators are narrow, explainable, deterministic, and tied to constitutional/package boundaries. | Fixture-backed positive/negative validation behaviour. | Pre-production validation capability. |
| Campaign 0 repository evidence | Test production against repository evidence. | Produced repository-level evidence packages and rejection evidence. | Existing contracts can handle production artifacts as evidence. | Determinism, fingerprints, rejected candidates, quality reporting. | Production validation began. |
| WDI demographic maturation | Mature first evidence family. | Campaigns 1-7 produced evidence-quality, completeness, freshness, inventory, territorial, temporal, and provenance-lineage knowledge. | Mature status requires multiple scopes, provenance-lineage completeness, rejected-candidate evidence, and family closeout. | WDI Demographic Family Closeout Report. | First Mature production family. |
| Second-family selection and transfer | Test methodology beyond one family. | Selected WDI Environment; transferred evidence-quality and production workflow. | Family transfer must preserve architecture and earn maturity independently. | Campaign 8 transfer evidence without taxonomy/validator/package pressure. | Second-family maturation began. |
| Cross-family and recurrence audit | Exercise multi-reference and recurrence assumptions. | Campaign 9 proved multi-reference cross-family objects; Campaign 10 audited recurrence and duplicate pressure. | Multi-reference objects fit existing package contract; duplicate registry not justified. | Campaign 9/10 accepted/rejected packages and PEL updates. | Remaining falsification gaps narrowed. |
| WDI Environment maturation | Complete second Mature family. | Campaigns 11-12 completed Environment inventory/coverage/provenance-lineage and closeout. | Family maturity criteria transfer unchanged. | WDI Environment Family Closeout Report. | Second Mature production family. |
| Production methodology validation | Validate method across families. | Production Methodology Closeout Report documented package, validator, support, provenance, fingerprint, rejected-candidate, maturity, and PEL stability. | Architecture changes require repeated production evidence showing insufficiency. | Two Mature WDI annual-scalar families. | Production methodology validated. |
| Phase 2 strategy | Determine long-term expansion sequence. | Recommended balanced expansion beginning with WDI Infrastructure. | Production family ordering should maximize reusable objective knowledge, not economic importance. | Phase 2 Production Expansion Strategy. | Operational expansion path selected. |
| Production Doctrine Freeze | Make validated methodology inherited doctrine. | Created `docs/production_doctrine.md`, freeze report, inheritance spec, operational guidance. | Future families inherit doctrine by default; deviations require repeated production evidence. | Campaigns 0-12, two closeouts, methodology closeout, stable PEL. | Operational doctrine frozen. |
| Phase 1 closeout | Preserve history and formally transition eras. | Phase 1 closeout, timeline, historical preservation, Operational Expansion declaration. | Foundation artifacts become historical references; operational docs remain living. | Closeout verification and updated state/roadmap/continuity. | Operational Expansion begins. |
