# KnowledgeForge Architectural Assimilation Decision Report

Date: 2026-07-09
Status: completed

## Decision basis

Each decision was evaluated against KnowledgeForge's constitutional purpose and the special-focus lens: reproducibility, reduced frontier LLM usage, deterministic computation, local AI feasibility, auditability, provenance, and maintainability.

## Adopt

1. Correct project-specific constitution.
   - Justification: root constitutional text must identify KnowledgeForge, not ProjectForge. This was corrected immediately because constitutional authority drift is a blocking inconsistency.
2. Summary-first context, current-state pointers, and standard closeout.
   - Justification: improves recovery and token efficiency without altering knowledge boundaries.
3. Context audit before frontier/cloud governance escalation.
   - Justification: directly reduces unnecessary frontier LLM use and creates audit trail.
4. Architecture-to-reality audit cadence.
   - Justification: prevents divergence among specification, fixtures, validators, and state.
5. Automation restraint doctrine.
   - Justification: KnowledgeForge should automate only when deterministic, reversible, observable, and maintainable.
6. Evidence-only slices.
   - Justification: allows architectural evidence accumulation without accidental implementation authorization.
7. Knowledge/evidence separation.
   - Justification: central to KnowledgeForge's purpose and already aligned with invariants.
8. Snapshots/immutable references over continuous mirrors.
   - Justification: improves reproducibility and avoids unnecessary infrastructure.
9. Optional materialization.
   - Justification: extracted knowledge plus provenance should be usable without requiring local caches.
10. Repeated evidence before abstraction promotion.
    - Justification: prevents premature ontological or framework generalization.
11. Contradiction preservation.
    - Justification: already constitutional; should become explicit artifact discipline.
12. Advisory-only consumer boundary.
    - Justification: downstream use is not evidence and must not mutate KnowledgeForge state.

## Adapt

1. ProjectForge five-system model.
   - Adapt as an audit lens, not as KnowledgeForge's domain architecture.
2. Validator classification.
   - Adapt categories to KnowledgeForge: constitutional, knowledge-model, repository, governance, tooling/environment, compatibility.
3. Terminology governance.
   - Adapt for claim facets, evidence states, lifecycle states, dependency facets, confidence labels, and mapping semantics.
4. ProjectForge canonization workflow.
   - Adapt before adding any reusable KnowledgeForge subsystem or generator.
5. MacroForge source-specific-before-boundary architecture.
   - Adapt as evidence-family-specific extraction before a common knowledge-candidate boundary.
6. MacroForge ObservedIngestionPackage.
   - Adapt conceptually into a future fingerprintable KnowledgeObjectPackage/KnowledgeChangePackage.
7. MacroForge fingerprinting, idempotent reruns, run-scoped validation, lineage, drift checks, and feedback reports.
   - Adapt to knowledge objects, evidence contracts, and knowledge-change records.
8. MacroForge capability maturity and DRDF/ACPF/CEF planning.
   - Adapt to object-type/evidence-family/generation-pipeline readiness and bounded knowledge campaigns.
9. MetaHarvest evidence-source taxonomy and lifecycle.
   - Adapt to KnowledgeForge evidence references and evidence evaluations.
10. MetaHarvest research cycle and knowledge evolution report.
    - Adapt into candidate/evaluation/contradiction/lifecycle/change completion discipline.
11. MetaHarvest component cards and problem-first retrieval.
    - Adapt into compact object/evidence/evaluation cards and question/claim-first retrieval.
12. Reusable prompt/template workflows.
    - Adapt only as validated intermediate-candidate generation, never as authority.

## Reject

1. PostgreSQL as default knowledge store by analogy.
   - Reason: MacroForge's storage success solves observational data requirements, not necessarily versioned knowledge-object requirements.
2. Runtime graph/vector/dashboard/UI/scheduler infrastructure now.
   - Reason: high complexity; no current evidence that file-backed packages and validators are insufficient.
3. External architecture harvesting inside KnowledgeForge.
   - Reason: that is MetaHarvest's role and would violate KnowledgeForge's domain boundary.
4. Autonomous knowledge-generation daemon.
   - Reason: unacceptable provenance and quality risk before object/evidence/gate architecture exists.
5. Generic provider/domain/framework extraction now.
   - Reason: MacroForge evidence shows extraction should follow repeated convergence, not precede it.
6. Consumer adoption as evidence or lifecycle state.
   - Reason: downstream use does not increase truth/confidence.

## Defer

1. Local-model extraction pilots.
   - Defer until evidence-source, package, validator, and template contracts exist.
2. PostgreSQL interaction patterns for empirical claims.
   - Defer until storage criteria and MacroForge evidence-reference contract are accepted.
3. Numeric maturity/confidence scoring.
   - Defer until repeated evidence supports calibrated metrics; use explainable labels first.
4. Pattern-centric retrieval infrastructure.
   - Defer runtime implementation; preserve compatibility through provenance and indexes.
5. Broad knowledge-generation pipelines.
   - Defer until a single narrow reproducible pipeline has been designed and verified.

## Immediate correction performed

`CONSTITUTION.md` was corrected from a ProjectForge constitution to a KnowledgeForge constitution. This is documentation/governance correction, not implementation expansion.
