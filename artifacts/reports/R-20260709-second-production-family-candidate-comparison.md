## Evaluation notes

1. **Environment** best balances novelty and control. It is non-demographic, WDI-native, annual-scalar-compatible, moderately sized, and suitable for objective evidence-level knowledge.
2. **Infrastructure** is the best fallback if Environment evidence proves unexpectedly awkward during Campaign 8 preflight, but fallback should require concrete preflight evidence.
3. **Education** and **Health** are viable later families, but they are either too large or too semantically adjacent for the first broadening step.
4. **Trade** and **Financial Sector** are not rejected permanently. They are deferred because their first campaign has a larger boundary-risk surface.

## Decision

Proceed with WDI Environment annual-scalar evidence as the second production family.
