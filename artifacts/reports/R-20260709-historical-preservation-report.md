# Historical Preservation Report — Foundation Era

Date: 2026-07-09
Status: historical preservation report

## 1. Purpose

This report identifies completed Foundation Era artifacts that should now be treated as historical references rather than living operational documents.

Historical reference means: future work may cite them for rationale, evidence, and continuity, but should not continuously revise their conclusions except for factual correction, broken link repair, or explicit supersession notes.

Operational documents are not archived here; they remain living doctrine/governance/state artifacts.
