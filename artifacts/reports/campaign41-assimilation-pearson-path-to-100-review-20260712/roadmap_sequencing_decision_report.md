# Roadmap Sequencing Decision Report

## Selected option

E. Use a mixed roadmap:

- limited raw Pearson baseline;
- transformation-aware relationships;
- richer deterministic diagnostics;
- no arbitrary object-count target as the primary success measure.

## Why not A or F

Default preservation of the existing roadmap was considered. The 21-object evidence is now sufficient to change sequencing because Campaign 41 produced 8/8 high-time-risk objects, and several strong raw correlations substantially weaken in first differences. Continuing unchanged toward 100 would likely increase object count faster than reusable knowledge value.

## Why not C alone

Transformation-aware Pearson should be validated before further large raw expansion, but raw Pearson remains useful as a bounded baseline descriptor and cautionary knowledge family. A pure pause is too narrow.

## Why not D alone

Other deterministic families such as statistical summaries, covariance structures, lag relationships, trend descriptors, and reusable mathematical relationships remain valuable. But the immediate evidence points to a mixed relationship roadmap rather than abandoning raw Pearson entirely.

## Production-evidence justification

- 14/21 Pearson objects have numeric time-index diagnostics; all 14 are high risk under review-only descriptive rubric.
- 7/21 older objects lack numeric time-index diagnostics/first-difference values, making their warning precision weaker for downstream consumers.
- 5 objects meet the review-only strong-raw/weak-first-difference pattern.
- Campaign 41 specifically demonstrates semantically remote cross-family candidates can become cautionary rather than positive relationship knowledge.

## Decision

Raw Pearson path-to-100 should not remain the primary operational target. Preserve existing accepted packages, preserve raw Pearson as a limited baseline descriptor, and sequence the next work toward mixed deterministic relationship utility.

Smallest next task: write a bounded Pearson candidate-policy refinement specification that adds semantic-proximity and time-risk stratification to coefficient-free registry construction, without calculating Campaign 42, implementing a new method, mutating packages, or changing schema/doctrine.
