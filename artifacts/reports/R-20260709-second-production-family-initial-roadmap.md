# Initial Production Roadmap — WDI Environment Annual-Scalar Evidence Family

Date: 2026-07-09
Status: proposed roadmap for second production family
Selected family: WDI Environment annual-scalar evidence

## Roadmap principle

Model the roadmap after the successful WDI demographic family only where the production-methodology logic transfers. Do not copy the demographic sequence mechanically.

The second family should first test cross-family transfer at low risk, then add coverage richness, then provenance-lineage depth, then cross-family comparison.

## Campaign 8 — WDI Environment evidence-quality and coverage transfer

Objective: test whether the existing deterministic WDI production method transfers from demographic evidence to Environment evidence while producing only evidence-quality, coverage, factual, classified, negative, provenance, and methodological knowledge.

Expected capabilities:

- Environment SourceEvidencePackage construction using the existing Production Support layer.
- Evidence-quality and basic coverage Knowledge Objects.
- Rejected-candidate preservation for malformed provenance, unsupported categories, and boundary-language failures.
- Deterministic replay and fingerprint stability.

Expected falsification opportunities:

- category consistency across non-demographic WDI evidence;
- whether validator paths are overfit to demographic wording;
- whether source-family scope transfer causes package-field ambiguity;
- whether object similarity produces duplicate pressure.

## Campaign 9 — Cross-family WDI annual-scalar coverage comparison

Objective: compare demographic and Environment evidence-family coverage using only inventory-level and evidence-quality facts, with no interpretation or significance claims.

Expected capabilities:

- multi-reference Knowledge Objects using one demographic evidence package and one Environment evidence package;
- deterministic comparative inventory knowledge;
- dependency/evidence-reference field stress;
- cross-family duplicate and overlap checks.

Expected falsification opportunities:

- PEL-012 multi-reference accepted objects;
- PEL-017 partial overlap and comparison-pressure assumptions;
- boundary rejection for significance, causal, or recommendation wording.

## Campaign 10 — WDI Environment indicator-family inventory and matrix coverage

Objective: mature Environment-specific inventory, territorial coverage, temporal coverage, and missingness knowledge after cross-family transfer is validated.

Expected capabilities:

- environment-family inventory;
- supported/unsupported dimensions;
- territorial/temporal coverage matrices;
- deterministic derived coverage buckets;
- scoped negative knowledge for missingness.

Expected falsification opportunities:

- object volume outside demographics;
- larger deterministic transformations;
- repeated missingness outside demographics;
- maturity criteria portability.

## Campaign 11 — WDI Environment provenance-lineage completeness and family maturity gate

Objective: test provenance-lineage completeness for Environment evidence and decide whether the Environment family has reached Mature status.

Expected capabilities:

- raw artifact identity;
- source identity;
- release metadata;
- provenance envelopes;
- lineage/fingerprint validation;
- family maturity assessment and closeout report if Mature.

Expected falsification opportunities:

- whether Campaign 7 provenance-lineage lessons transfer;
- whether family closeout criteria are reusable;
- whether PEL-020 can move from campaign-specific toward evidence-family recurrence.

## Maturity criteria for the second family

The WDI Environment family should be considered Mature only if it demonstrates:

1. deterministic replay and fingerprint stability across multiple Environment campaigns;
2. provenance completeness and lineage/fingerprint validation;
3. rejected-candidate preservation with meaningful failure categories;
4. no required taxonomy, validator, package-model, or architecture change;
5. scoped negative knowledge where missingness or unsupported dimensions exist;
6. at least one inventory/classification scope and one coverage/matrix scope;
7. explicit cross-family comparison evidence with demographic production;
8. at least one accepted multi-reference Knowledge Object or a documented evidence-backed reason why the multi-reference assumption must move to a later multi-source family.

## Non-goals

No architecture redesign, validator modification, ontology/taxonomy change, runtime infrastructure, adapters/APIs/shared schemas, database coupling, local model generation, frontier model generation, domain interpretation, economic meaning, policy meaning, investment meaning, recommendations, or presentation narrative.
