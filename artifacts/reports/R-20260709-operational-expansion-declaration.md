# Operational Expansion Declaration

Date: 2026-07-09
Status: active era declaration

## 1. Declaration

KnowledgeForge Operational Expansion begins now.

The Foundation Era is closed. The validated production methodology is inherited doctrine. Future work should primarily expand objective, reproducible, evidence-backed knowledge through production families.

## 2. Meaning of Operational Expansion

Operational Expansion means:

1. Production families are the primary development activity.
2. Architecture work becomes exceptional rather than routine.
3. Governance evolves only through production evidence.
4. Production methodology is inherited by default.
5. Deviations require repeated production evidence showing inherited doctrine insufficiency.
6. Planning exists to scope family production, not to redesign doctrine.
7. Rejected candidates, validation failures, closeout reports, and PEL observations remain the evidence basis for future change.

## 3. What does not change

Operational Expansion does not change KnowledgeForge's constitution, architecture, package contracts, validators, taxonomy, provenance model, fingerprint model, production methodology, Production Support boundary, or Production Evolution Log governance.

It does not authorize runtime infrastructure, repository coupling, adapters, APIs, shared schemas, database coupling, local LLM generation, or frontier LLM generation.

## 4. Immediate operational direction

Begin Phase 2 by maturing the WDI Infrastructure annual-scalar production family using `docs/production_doctrine.md` unchanged.

The next family should inherit:

- package model;
- validator model;
- production workflow;
- provenance handling;
- fingerprinting;
- reporting;
- PEL governance;
- maturity assessments;
- family closeout process.

## 5. Burden of proof

Future recommendations should assume the doctrine is correct unless production evidence demonstrates otherwise.

Preference, convenience, abstraction, novelty, anticipated reuse, external-project similarity, model availability, or dashboard/API/database convenience are not sufficient reasons to deviate.

## 6. Classification

Operational Expansion Declaration classification: preserves agreed architecture.
