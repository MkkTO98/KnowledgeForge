# D-20260801 — Two-Evidence-Portfolio Generalization Review v1

Date: 2026-08-01
Status: accepted review conclusion; bounded successor recommended but not activated
Decision class: empirical framework-generalization classification

## Decision

Classify the current Evidence Portfolio model as:

`provisionally generalizable with bounded corrections`

This is the strongest conclusion supported by Norway Health and Sweden Infrastructure. It is intentionally weaker than universal or production-scale generality.

## Demonstrated

Across both portfolios, the accepted model supported:

- outcome-blind family selection and fingerprinted preregistration;
- explicit territory, period, unit, observational-population, denominator and applicability metadata;
- one pre-execution applicability exclusion;
- canary-first execution and content-bound conditional continuation;
- deterministic 28-record baseline characterization per series;
- stable identity, exact retained-input lineage and immutable-input checks;
- package validation, append-only canonical promotion and evolution records;
- explicit separation of canonical Knowledge Objects from operational Evidence-Card-equivalent views;
- complete raw/valid/promoted/view/bundle/dependency/exclusion/null/redundancy/failure accounting;
- reproducible reruns and canonical-repository authentication;
- compatibility with the existing rebuildable PostgreSQL projection;
- content-adequate direct package/projection consumption without recalculation.

Exact evidence is enumerated in `artifacts/reports/R-20260801-two-evidence-portfolio-generalization-review-v1.md`.

## Only suggested or untested

The evidence only suggests that explicit family configuration can support further annual-scalar baseline families. It does not establish generality across providers, methods, frequencies, missingness patterns, disagreement, runtime failures, supersession, structural breaks, relationship claims, causal or predictive methods, large portfolios, or a general InsightForge adapter.

Two completed portfolios are also not independent replications: both use retained Campaign 40 WDI fixtures, the same production engine, statistical-summary method, canonical repository, projection, candidate limits, and operational-view path.

## Observed corrections required before a third portfolio

Authorize a separately bounded successor review/implementation task—without changing either existing portfolio or any canonical object—to:

1. make `excluded_pre_execution`, execution rejection, null, redundancy and execution failure separate exact accounting categories across manifest, machine accounting, task and report surfaces;
2. require narrative candidate descriptions to bind to manifest candidate identity and exclusion reason, preventing the documented CAGR-versus-all-pairs drift;
3. require one stable transformation identifier from manifest through result records, views and projection evidence;
4. require an explicit bounded analytical question/intended-use field and candidate-to-question traceability;
5. distinguish source-series clusters from shared provider/acquisition/method dependence so distinct series cannot be misread as independent corroboration.

These are bounded conformance and reporting corrections grounded in observed cross-portfolio discrepancies. They are not authorization for a new ontology, parallel portfolio subsystem, package rewrite, canonical migration, or advanced statistics.

## Single successor path

Select option 1: **a bounded framework correction before further portfolio production**.

Suggested task name, not activated:

`Evidence Portfolio accounting, traceability and dependence conformance correction v1`

The task should change only prospective portfolio contracts/tooling/tests/templates as necessary, retain both existing portfolios as immutable regression fixtures, and prove exact failure on narrative/manifest, disposition, transformation and dependence mismatches.

## Most discriminating later third-portfolio criteria

After that correction, the most informative third portfolio would be deliberately divergent rather than convenient. It should use already admitted durable evidence and differ from both current portfolios on several dimensions:

- a non-WDI provider or genuinely independent admitted source family;
- a denominator with eligibility/exposure rather than total resident-population scaling;
- incomplete or irregular temporal coverage with real alignment decisions;
- a different observational frequency or a justified nonannual grain;
- plausible source or estimate disagreement;
- a known applicability break or definitional change;
- at least one expected null, rejection, or negative-control path;
- a decision-use question that remains descriptive and does not require causal or predictive inference.

This defines selection criteria only. It does not select, pre-register, calculate, or activate a third portfolio.

## Other change disposition

Valuable but non-blocking:

- quantify reviewer time and exception count, not only compute time;
- add first-class projection filters only when a concrete consumer requires them;
- make isolated-clone fixture topology hermetic or explicitly provisioned.

Deferred until more diverse evidence exists:

- general InsightForge baseline adapter;
- structural-break or regime methods;
- multi-method triangulation and disagreement synthesis;
- supersession behavior for portfolio-produced objects;
- scale claims beyond the current four canonical objects and 112 operational views.

Rejected as unnecessary complexity:

- parallel portfolio or batching architecture;
- a second database architecture;
- a new canonical Evidence Card ontology;
- output padding toward 100 or 1,000;
- broad relationship, graph, causal, forecasting, or advanced-statistics expansion.

## Portability classification

The isolated-clone full-suite failure is not a semantic KnowledgeForge failure. It is a KnowledgeForge-owned test-portability/fixture-topology defect masked by the normal sibling EIP layout: `tests/test_external_outbox_polling_supersession_v1.py` defaults to a MacroForge sibling export and unconditionally copies it. MacroForge durably owns the producer export; shared EIP topology is environmental; KnowledgeForge owns making its test contract hermetic or explicitly provisionable. This is valuable but does not block the bounded pre-third accounting/traceability correction.

## Campaign 43 clarification

`constructed_not_published` refers to the Campaign 43 preflight operation that constructed and validated six packages in a temporary repository without canonical or projection publication. It is not the final status of Campaign 43. The later accepted decision `D-20260712-campaign43-canonical-publication-accepted.md` records the separate canonical publication of those six packages.

## Non-claims

This decision does not claim:

- universal portfolio generality;
- independent corroboration from four source series;
- analytical or causal validity beyond bounded descriptive sequence summaries;
- empirical coverage of natural null, redundant, failed, contradictory or missing-data outcomes;
- readiness for indiscriminate 100- or 1,000-output production;
- InsightForge adapter compatibility;
- authorization to construct a third portfolio or begin the successor correction.
