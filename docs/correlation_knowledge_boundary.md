# Correlation Knowledge Boundary

A correlation Knowledge Object belongs to KnowledgeForge only when it reports a deterministic mathematical relationship between explicitly scoped evidence series.

It may contain exact series identities, entity or entity-pair scope, frequency, aligned period window, transformation state, aligned observation count, missingness/exclusion information, deterministic correlation coefficient, method/calculation-contract fingerprints, evidence fingerprints, provenance, and limitations.

It may not claim causation, explanation, mechanism, predictive power, economic significance, statistical significance, stability outside the measured window, investment relevance, recommendation, meaningful lead/lag relationships, trend, stationarity, or independence of observations.

InsightForge may later interpret correlations. KnowledgeForge only preserves the reproducible relationship and scope.
